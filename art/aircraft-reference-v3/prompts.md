# Aircraft v3 prompts

All six assets were generated with the built-in image generator as transparent right-facing drafts, then losslessly mirrored to make every aircraft face left. Each prompt requested a side profile, full aircraft, no logo, no text, no watermark, and no background, ground, or shadow. The shared style is clean stylized 2D game art with a white upper fuselage, sky-blue underside, gold trim, and deep-blue accents.

Passenger prompts:

- Light: compact twin-engine turboprop passenger airliner, landing gear down, clear windows and cockpit.
- Regional: medium narrow-body twin-jet passenger airliner with under-wing engines and extended wings.
- Heavy: large wide-body passenger jet with long fuselage, high tail, and four under-wing engines.

Cargo prompts:

- Light: compact twin-engine turboprop freighter, utilitarian fuselage, and cargo door details.
- Regional: medium narrow-body cargo jet with few windows, reinforced skin, and front cargo door details.
- Heavy: large four-engine wide-body freighter with long fuselage, few windows, and reinforced cargo body.

Runtime assets are trimmed and scaled onto a 1200x540 transparent canvas with the aircraft bottom-anchored. Target subject heights are 320px for light, 370px for regional, and about 440px for heavy aircraft; heavy cargo was width-limited to 1140px.
